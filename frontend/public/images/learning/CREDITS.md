# Learning photography

Downloaded 2026-10-22 for the VEDU homepage. These are photographic stock images,
not AI-generated illustrations. Original source pages include named photographers,
2020/2021 publication/capture dates and camera metadata. Only the original photos
are used, not the site's AI edit suggestions. WebP delivery is resized/compressed
by Pexels; visual cropping is performed in CSS.

Photos illustrate learning situations. The people pictured are not identified as
VEDU staff, registered learners or endorsers. Do not add fictional names, course
ratings, testimonials or faculty credentials to these photographs.

Delivery sources use `https://images.pexels.com/photos/PHOTO_ID/pexels-photo-PHOTO_ID.jpeg`
with `auto=compress&cs=tinysrgb&w=WIDTH&fm=webp`. Widths are 1200, 640, 640 and
1000 respectively. Files are local so homepage photos do not require a third-party
image request or Internet connection at runtime.

`study-books.svg` and `study-laptop.svg` are original VEDU decorative vector
illustrations, drawn for the homepage. They contain no photographs or third-party
logos and do not represent actual people.
